# Logo

`egui-pop.svg` is egui-pop's logo, 512 by 512.

It is drawn from egui's own icon, [`crates/egui_demo_lib/data/icon.svg`](https://github.com/emilk/egui/blob/main/crates/egui_demo_lib/data/icon.svg):

- the hexagon and its cross are that icon's path, unchanged;
- the hexagon is filled with pink and cream twisted stripes;
- a black rim, a third of a branch of the cross wide, cuts a stick that runs along the diagonal and out of the square.

`egui-pop-badge.svg` is the README's badge, after egui's own: our logo where egui's icon sits, egui's wordmark unchanged in white, and "-pop" in the logo's pink. Its p and o are cut from egui's g: the p is the g mirrored with its stem taken down to the descender, the o is the g's bowl and its mirror image.
