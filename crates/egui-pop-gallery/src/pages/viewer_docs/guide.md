# The guide

A page is markdown: **strong**, *emphasis*, `code` and [links](index.md).

## Navigation

Back and forward walk the pages seen in this session; the address bar
shows where the page comes from and opens what you type.

| Key | What |
|---|---|
| Alt+Left | Back |
| Alt+Right | Forward |
| F5 | Reload |

## Find in page

Ctrl+F searches the page; F3 and Shift+F3 move between the matches.

## Zoom

Ctrl+plus and Ctrl+minus scale the page, Ctrl+0 brings it back.
