A switch: `widgets::switch(ui, &mut on, text)` or `widgets::Switch`, an on/off toggle with a track (the kit's new `switch` component) and a thumb, a switch to assistive technology, toggled by a click, Space or Enter. Inline emphasis: `ui.strong(text)` and `ui::strong_text`, the body's size in a heavier weight (the `text-role.strong` tokens). Fixed sizes: `ui.sized(size, widget)`, `ui.sized_ui(size, layout, add)` and `ui.rect_ui(rect, layout, add)` replace egui's `add_sized`, `allocate_ui_with_layout` and `put`.
