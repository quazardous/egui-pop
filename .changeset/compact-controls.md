Compact controls for dense rows, where a control has a slot of a given size and no label beside it (a HUD's card, a property sheet): `Slider::scrub` draws the slider as a scrub field (the label and the value in a plain field, no fill and no thumb; every gesture, key, menu and name stays the slider's); `Input::cell` fits the decorated input exactly into a size, with the small padding; `compact::ChoiceCell` is a choice sized to a slot (a word longer than the slot is cut to it), named for assistive technologies, Up and Down choosing while its list is closed; `compact::MenuRow` is a row the width of its room in the look of a menu's items, with a muted note at its end, a button pressed by a click, Space or Enter. `Indicator::mark` draws a value's marks (`ValueMark::Changed`, `Held`, `Moved`): a dot, a square and a diamond, each with its meaning in English and French ("Changed from its default", "Held by …", "Moved by …"), recorded for agents as kind `mark`. `Meter::size` and `Meter::vertical` make the meter a value bar filling a slot, across or upright (vertical for assistive technologies too); the kit's `tick` shape draws along a part's longer side. The gallery's "Compact controls" page shows them.
