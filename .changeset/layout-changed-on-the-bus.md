The layout's changes are said on the bus: every real change of the workbench's layout (a panel docked, detached, floated, moved, opened or closed, a split, a palette or a flyout resized, a detached window moved, a layout put in) is said once as `layout.changed` (`bus::LayoutChanged`, a `bus::LayoutChange` naming its causes), once the gesture that made it ended: a drag or a resize in progress says nothing until it is let go, a window or a palette following the system's moves once it stayed still for `bus::LAYOUT_SETTLE`, and a step that changed nothing is not said. The layout is saved by its listener `layout.save` (`bus::LAYOUT_SAVE`, through the `layout.save` step, to the document the host names) at once, instead of a comparison of the layout every pass and a debounce; an application keeping a copy of the layout elsewhere listens after it. A layout loaded is not said. The point is in `bus::CONTRACT` and `bus::POINTS` (now 21 points, a breaking change for code naming the array's length; `bus::LAYOUT_POINTS` lists it).
