Four bricks for layout code, experimental: `UiPop::code` (monospace text in the body's colour, for a log or console line), `UiPop::text_tone` (the body role in a tone's colour from the tokens, with `ui::tone_color` for the mapping), `UiPop::divider` (a rule in `border.default` between two sections, upright in a row) and `compact::RowButton` (a button small enough for a list's row, named by its words, focused by Tab, pressed with Space or Enter). The ui-index's text-role line now names the roles that exist.
