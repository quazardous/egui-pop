Quick settings (`admin.quick`, `Ctrl+Shift+Q`, File > Quick settings): a small panel over the window it is asked in that edits settings without opening Settings. It lists the settings manifests mark quick (`Manifest::quick`), then those changed lately; typing searches every setting. Each row is the Settings app's own row, with "Set for" only on a setting that has a host level, and a link to its page.
