The shell's windows are on the bus (ADR 0029, slice 4, first part). `AppHandle::open_window` is a `window.open` request owned by the application (`app`); `AppHandle::request_window_open` (Experimental) opens one under an owner of its own. The main window's close, whatever asks it (the system, its title bar's Close, `close_window` of the main window, a content moved into it closing), is one `window.close` of the main window: a before-hook on the `window.close` step may veto it (the system's close is then cancelled and nothing changes), and with another window open the window worked in last moves into the main window instead, as a `window.reparent`. Raising the main window while a window's content holds it moves that content back to its own window, a `window.reparent` with no parent (`AppHandle::request_window_reparent`, Experimental; `bus::STEP_WINDOW_REPARENT`). `window.reparent` runs `window.registry`, `window.place`, `window.focus` and the invariants, in that declared order; `window.moved` and the `window.closed` / `window.opened` it said before are said after them. `AppHandle::quit` is the main window's `window.close` with the reason `quit`: each other window is closed first by its own `window.close` (`quit`), its owner's state with it, and a veto leaves the application as it was. Closing the main window for good closes each other window the same way (`parent-closed`). The main window's registry entry leaves when it closes.
