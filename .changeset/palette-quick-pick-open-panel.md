The command palette shows quick picks: `Palette::pick_in` opens it on a
`QuickPick`, a list of choices filtered by the same fuzzy match, each
running a command with its arguments. "Open panel…"
(`workbench.panel.pick`, `Ctrl+Shift+O`) is the first: every declared
panel, the one chosen opened where it was (`workbench.panel.open`,
`place: last`), in English and French.
