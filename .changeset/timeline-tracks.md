Tracks and clips on the timeline (`views::timeline`): `TimelineState::tracks` holds rows of `Clip`s (a start, a length, an offset into a source, a label, the source's name and length, a colour) under the ruler, never overlapping on a track, and `TimelineDoc` keeps them, so each change is a step of the history. A `ClipPainter` given with `Timeline::clips` draws a clip's content (a video's thumbnails, an audio waveform, a data plot; a closure is one too), the timeline its box, selection and trimming handles; `PlainClips`, the default, draws the labels. A click selects a clip (Ctrl toggles, Shift adds), a drag moves the selection along a track or across tracks, landing against the nearest edge where it would overlap, and a clip's edges trim it, stopping at its source's ends and its neighbours; clips snap to one another's edges (`Snap::clips`, `ruler::snap_span`). Keys: Page Up and Page Down walk a track's clips, Shift+Up and Shift+Down reach the clip at the playhead on the track above or below, Ctrl+Left and Ctrl+Right move the selection, Ctrl+Up and Ctrl+Down to another track, `[` and `]` trim to the playhead, Delete removes. `TimelineState` gains `add_track`, `add_clip`, `move_clips`, `trim_clip` and `remove_clips` (refusals as `ClipError`), and `Edit` the clip edits (it is now non-exhaustive). Each clip is an accessible button named with its track and times. Agents get `timeline.add_clip`, `move_clip`, `trim_clip`, `remove_clip` and `select_clips`, and the `timeline` collector lists the tracks and clips. New tokens `timeline.track.*` and `timeline.clip.*`. The gallery has a Tracks and clips page.

Migration: `Snap`, `TimelineState`, `TimelineDoc`, `TimelineTokens` and `TimelineOutput` have new fields (`clips`; `tracks` and `selected_clips`; `tracks`; the track and clip colours and `track_height`; `tracks`): a struct literal needs them or `..Default::default()` (`Snap { grid: false, ..Snap::default() }`). `ruler::Snapped` has a `Clip` variant, and a `match` on `Edit` needs a `_` arm. `Timeline::lanes` is now the height under the tracks, the same as before when there are none.
