The library's comparison (`views::library::compare`) is one surface for one to many images: one image is a loupe filling the surface, with the same zoom, pan and wheel as the image viewport; two to four sit side by side as before (four in a square), and more tile in the grid that shows them the largest (six in three columns), up to `CompareView::max_panes` (four by default, at most `PANE_LIMIT`, 16). The application keeps owning the list: the surface says what the user asked, as `Intent`s in `CompareOutput::intents`: a pane made active (a click, the arrows, Home and End, the keyboard focus), images dropped on it to add at a place, a pane taken out (its close button and Delete or Backspace on the active pane, with `CompareView::removable`), a pane moved. With `CompareView::carry`, images come in and go out through the application's drag session, across its windows too: a drag of the application's payload let go on the surface adds its images (a bar shows where, a full comparison refuses with its reason), a pane's name drags its image out, and a pane let go over nothing goes out. `CompareView::active` outlines the active image; `CompareOutput::panes` says where each pane was drawn, and `compare::layout` gives the same places. Each pane is a list item named after its image and selected when active, inside a list named Compare that takes the keyboard. Breaking: `CompareOutput` is `#[non_exhaustive]` and has new fields; `CompareState` keeps one view per pane for any count, with `removed`, `inserted` and `moved` to follow the application's list. New strings: `egui-pop-views-compare-empty`, `-compare-remove`, `-compare-full`, `-compare-not-images` (English and French).
