An application window's close is one request on the bus (ADR 0029). `egui-pop-api` holds a bus (`AppHandle::bus`; the application may supply its own, see the bus contract) with the window and popup points (`bus::WindowOpen`, `WindowClose`, `WindowReparent`, `PopupOpen`, `PopupClose`, each taking a `bus::WindowRequest`: window, kind, owner, reason, cause). Closing an application window, whoever asks (its title bar, its content, `window.close`, `AppHandle::close_window`, `AppHandle::request_window_close`), runs the `window.close` step and then its listeners in one call, in their declared order: `window.children` (its dialogs), the owner's (`admin.window`: the Settings app closes with its window), `window.focus`, `window.registry`, `window.place`, `window.invariants` (last); `window.closed` (with owner and reason) is said after the last one, and `window.refused` when vetoed or unknown. The Settings app in its own window no longer closes in two steps (its window at once, its state at the main window's next pass, up to two seconds later), and a pass of a closed window run before the backend drops it draws the window's last page instead of nothing (a black window without its title bar on GNOME under Wayland). `AppHandle::windows_list` is the registry (`windows.list`); `AppHandle::bus_order` and `bus::order_dump` print the listeners in run order, also shown by the debug bridge's new `bus` collector, which lists every point on the bus, egui-pop's and the application's: its kind, who declared it (`by`: `egui_pop` from `bus::CONTRACT`, or `application`), whether it is declared, its description and its listeners in run order, with `missing_targets`. The hook kernel gains `ListenOpts::last` (`egui_pop_hooks::LAST`): one listener per point after every other, a second refused as a cycle. The test kit's `MultiWindow::painted` tells whether a window's last pass painted content.
