The job lifecycle is on the bus (ADR 0029). Asking about a job is a request: `job.cancel`, `job.pause`, `job.resume`, `job.retry` are points of the application's hook kernel (`bus::JobCancel`, `JobPause`, `JobResume`, `JobRetry`, each a `bus::JobAsk`), asked by `AppHandle::request_job` (and `jobs::ask`); their listeners run in the call in a declared order, `jobs.model` (the job shows as waiting for the answer) then `jobs.host` (its command runs for the host), and with nobody listening the request is refused as `job.refused`. The jobs model is the `jobs.model` listener of the `job.*` events (`bus::JobEvents`), which the bus delivers once each, in journal order, each pass and before a wait reads the jobs (`AppHandle::deliver_events`). A plugin's disconnection and connection are asked on the bus (`bus::PluginDisconnect`, `PluginConnect`, a `bus::PluginChange`), the disconnection before `plugin.disconnected` is said: `jobs.cancel-on-disconnect` cancels or orphans its jobs and counts them in the event, and `jobs.adopt-on-connect` says `job.adopted`, which the model now folds (`jobs::ADOPTED`, `Jobs::orphaned_by`). A disconnected plugin's listeners on the bus go with it. `job.orphaned` is now said before `plugin.disconnected`. `bus::JOB_POINTS`, `PLUGIN_POINTS`, `POINTS` and `AppHandle::bus_order_of` print the order. **Breaking** (experimental API): `JobsService::mark` and `JobsService::refine` are no longer public. Was `jobs.mark(id, request)`, now `jobs::ask` or `AppHandle::request_job` with the request; a refined estimate goes through the `jobs.estimate` step.
