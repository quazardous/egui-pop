`number::NumberInput`, a spin box: the value typed with its unit beside it, small up and down arrows, the arrow keys and the wheel stepping it within its range; a typed value out of range, a fraction for a whole number or a text that is not a number is refused with its reason. `param` (and so the settings form) draws an int or float whose range is wider than 1000 as one instead of a slider.
