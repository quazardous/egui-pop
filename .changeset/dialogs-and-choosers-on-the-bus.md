Dialogs and file choices are on the bus (ADR 0029, slice 5, first part). A dialog coming up is its `popup.open` (`dialog:<id>`, owned by the plugin that opened it or by `egui_pop.dialogs`, its parent the window of its group), and its answer, its cancel or its plugin going is its `popup.close`. A file choice started is its `popup.open` (`file:<id>`, owned by `egui_pop.files`, its parent the window it was asked from), or, for the system's chooser, the `window.open` of a native window; its end closes it. `popup.opened`, `popup.closed` and `popup.refused` are said for each. A window closing closes its popups and native windows first (`window.children`), each by its own request with the reason `parent-closed` (`quit` when the application quits), and their owners' listeners cancel the dialog or the choice in that call (`dialogs::POPUP_LISTENER`, `files::CHOICE_LISTENER`); the dialogs still waiting in its queue are cancelled by `dialogs::CANCEL_ON_CLOSE`, a listener of `window.close`. A closed window's dialogs and choices are no longer cancelled at a later pass. A window whose content moves into the main window takes its popups with it: they become the main window's. New: `AppHandle::request_popup_open`, `AppHandle::request_popup_close` and `AppHandle::request_native_open` (refused, and said so, without an owner or an open parent).
