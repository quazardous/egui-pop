A side area folds to its strip when its sash is dragged past half its least size, and a folded side area drags out of its strip's edge at the size it is dragged to, as in VS Code and JetBrains' IDEs. Unfolded, an area folded by drag has the size it had before. Sashes still do not snap to sizes, as in those applications.
